## A. Introduction
### Title: Interstellar
Interstellar is a science fiction film directed by Christopher Nolan that explores the survival of humanity in a dystopian future. The movie is an emotional journey across light-years as a group of astronauts ventures through a wormhole in search of a new home for mankind. In this movie, they combined real scientific concepts such as theoretical physics and general relativity with deeply human emotional arcs.

> Love is the one thing that we’re capable of perceiving that transcends dimensions of time and space. -Brand

Genre: Epic Science Fiction, Sci-Fi Drama, Adventure
Release Year: 2014


## B. Synopsis

In the mid-21st century, Earth is suffering from a global crop blight and severe dust storms, driving humanity toward extinction. Joseph Cooper, a former NASA test pilot turned farmer, discovers a hidden NASA facility after decoding gravitational anomalies found in his daughter Murphy's bedroom. 

Professor John Brand convinces Cooper to pilot the Endurance, a spacecraft sent through a wormhole near Saturn to explore potentially habitable planets in another galaxy. Cooper leaves behind his heartbroken children to save the human species. 

Along with a crew of scientists and tactical robots, Cooper faces extreme spatial and temporal challenges, including severe gravitational time dilation near a supermassive black hole named Gargantua. 

As time slips away on Earth, Murphy grows up to become a NASA scientist working to solve the gravitational gravity equation needed to evacuate Earth. Cooper must make ultimate sacrifices across spacetime to send critical data back to Murphy and secure a future for mankind.


## C. Characters

- Joseph Cooper
   A widowed ex-NASA pilot and engineer who is forced to farm to survive. He is driven by a deep love for his children and an unyielding passion for human space exploration.
- Murphy "Murph" Cooper
   Cooper’s intelligent and stubborn daughter. She grows up to become a brilliant NASA physicist working alongside Professor Brand to solve the gravity propulsion equation.
- Dr. Amelia Brand
   A passionate NASA scientist and astronaut aboard the *Endurance* mission. She believes in both scientific rigorousness and the transcendental power of love across space and time.
- TARS
   An articulate, box-shaped former military robot repurposed for the space mission. He provides vital technical support, tactical intelligence, and comedic relief through adjustable humor levels.
- Professor John Brand
   A high-ranking NASA director and head of Plan A. He devises the mathematical framework to transport Earth's population into space while secretly harbouring a darker contingency plan.


## D. Personal Review

### Why I Chose This Movie
I chose Interstellar because it seamlessly bridges hard physics with raw human emotion. It is a rare masterpiece that asks profound questions about time, sacrifice, and the lengths humanity will go to ensure its survival. I personally find it sublime, not just the concept, but the characters built throughout the film as well as the visual impact the movie has. To me it's one of the perfect films to ever be produced.

### 3 Things I Liked About It
1. Visual effects and scientific realism. The depiction of the supermassive black hole like *Gargantua* and the wormhole were designed using real physics equations in collaboration with theoretical physicist Kip Thorne.
2. The powerful soundtrack that elevates the tension and emotional depth of every scene, particularly during the intense docking sequence.
3. The emotional core. Despite its massive cosmic scale, the heart of the story remains the tender relationship between a father and his daughter separated by time and space.

### Personal Rating
Rating: 10 / 10

### Recommendation
I highly recommend Interstellar to anyone who enjoys thought-provoking cinema, space exploration, or emotional drama. Even if you are not a fan of hard sci-fi, its stunning visuals and emotional storytelling make it a must-watch cinematic masterpiece.
